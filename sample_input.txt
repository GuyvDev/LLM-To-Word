## Energy–Mass Equivalence

Einstein showed that energy and mass are related by:

$$E = mc^2$$

## Pythagorean Theorem

For any right triangle: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

The hypotenuse length is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.

## Calculus

## Roots of a Quadratic

When $a \neq 0$, the sum of roots is $x_1 + x_2 = \frac{-b}{a}$ and the product is $x_1 \cdot x_2 = \frac{c}{a}$.
